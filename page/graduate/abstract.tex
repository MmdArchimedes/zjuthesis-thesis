\chapternonum{摘要}

在”双碳”目标与数字化转型协同推进的背景下，数字经济对能源结构优化的作用机制备受关注。针对传统研究”计量分析充分而呈现与交互手段不足”的瓶颈，本文以中国省域数据为对象，构建面向经济能源分析的增强现实（Augmented Reality，AR）沉浸式分析系统，形成”多模态交互—沉浸式可视化—计量分析—应用验证”的一体化研究框架。

在交互层面，本文围绕AR穿戴场景提出两项核心技术。其一，设计深度骨骼约束下的边沿触发—时间窗离散化手势管线（DBEW--Gesture）及其轻量神经网络增强版本（DBEW-NN），以57K参数和1.11\,ms推理时延将手势识别宏平均F1由0.700提升至0.889，并完整验证了在Rokid AR Studio平台上的端侧部署可行性。其二，提出通道内稳定化—跨通道时间窗仲裁—队列串行提交（TSTQ--Fusion）的多模态融合机制，以置信度驱动的动态优先级仲裁实现手势、语音、射线三通道事件的统一收敛，仲裁正确率达96.4\%，冲突率仅3.3\%。上述两项技术通过置信度信号形成紧耦合：手势NN的置信度经触发管线过滤后注入融合评分函数，确保识别子系统与仲裁子系统在接口层面解耦、在决策层面协同。

在可视化层面，本文借鉴React声明式UI范式，提出ChartForge声明式图表生成框架。该框架包含五项核心技术：图表意图形式化表示（CIF）将自然语言需求映射为结构化图表规约；概率图表语法（PCG）以可学习概率文法描述12类图表的生成空间；语义-视觉对齐分数（SVAS）提供生成质量的定量度量；多阶段生成精炼管线（MS-GRP）通过”生成—评估—精炼”闭环迭代提升图表质量；可组合图表代数（CCA）支持图表的声明式组合与复用。在ChartIntent-10K测试集上，ChartForge的图表生成准确率达91.7\%，优于最佳基线9.2个百分点。

在应用验证层面，基于2014--2022年中国30个省份面板数据，以熵值法与熵权—决策树融合方法构建数字经济发展水平指数（DEL）与能源结构优化指数（ES），经面板固定效应基准回归、中介效应检验与区域异质性分析形成结构化数据资产，并通过AR沉浸式分析会话串联”全国格局把握—计量证据核对—分区差异讨论—省域下钻阅读”全流程，验证了系统的实际可用性。

研究表明，本文提出的技术方案有效贯通了”计量结论—空间表达—交互解读”的分析链路，为经济能源领域的沉浸式分析与决策支持提供了可复现、可部署的系统方案。

\textbf{关键词：}增强现实；多模态交互；手势识别；声明式图表生成；沉浸式可视化；数字经济；能源结构优化

\chapternonum{Abstract}

Against the backdrop of China's dual-carbon goals and digital transformation, the role of the digital economy in driving energy structure optimization has attracted considerable research attention. To address the bottleneck of “rigorous econometric analysis yet limited presentation and interaction capabilities” in existing work, this thesis builds an Augmented Reality (AR) immersive analytics system for provincial economic-energy analysis, forming an integrated framework of “multimodal interaction -- immersive visualization -- econometric analysis -- application validation.”

At the interaction level, two core techniques are proposed for wearable AR scenarios. First, a Depth-Bone Edge-triggered Windowing gesture pipeline (DBEW--Gesture) and its lightweight neural-network-enhanced variant (DBEW-NN) are designed. With only 57K parameters and 1.11\,ms inference latency, DBEW-NN improves gesture recognition macro-average F1 from 0.700 to 0.889, and its on-device deployment feasibility is fully validated on the Rokid AR Studio platform. Second, a Temporal Stabilization---Time-window Queuing Fusion mechanism (TSTQ--Fusion) is proposed, which employs confidence-driven dynamic priority arbitration to achieve unified convergence of gesture, speech, and ray-casting events, attaining 96.4\% arbitration accuracy with only 3.3\% conflict rate. These two techniques are tightly coupled through confidence signals: the NN confidence score, filtered by the trigger pipeline, is injected into the fusion scoring function, ensuring that the recognition and arbitration subsystems are decoupled at the interface level yet coordinated at the decision level.

At the visualization level, drawing on React's declarative UI paradigm, this thesis proposes ChartForge, a declarative chart generation framework comprising five core techniques: Chart Intent Formalization (CIF) maps natural-language requirements to structured chart specifications; Probabilistic Chart Grammar (PCG) describes the generation space of 12 chart types using a learnable probabilistic grammar; Semantic-Visual Alignment Score (SVAS) provides a quantitative metric for generation quality; Multi-Stage Generative Refinement Pipeline (MS-GRP) iteratively improves chart quality through a “generate--evaluate--refine” loop; and Composable Chart Algebra (CCA) supports declarative composition and reuse of chart elements. On the ChartIntent-10K benchmark, ChartForge achieves 91.7\% chart generation accuracy, outperforming the best baseline by 9.2 percentage points.

At the application validation level, using panel data from 30 Chinese provinces covering 2014--2022, a Digital Economy Level index (DEL) and an Energy Structure optimization index (ES) are constructed via entropy-weight and entropy-weighted decision tree fusion methods. Structured data assets are generated through fixed-effects baseline regression, mediation testing, and regional heterogeneity analysis. A complete AR immersive analysis session is conducted, connecting national-pattern overview, econometric evidence verification, regional comparison, and province-level drill-down reading, demonstrating the system's practical usability.

The results show that the proposed technical framework effectively bridges the chain of “econometric conclusions -- spatial presentation -- interactive exploration,” providing a reproducible and deployable system solution for immersive analytics and decision support in the energy-economy domain.

\textbf{Keywords:} augmented reality; multimodal interaction; gesture recognition; declarative chart generation; immersive visualization; digital economy; energy structure optimization
